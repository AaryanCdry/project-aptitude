An express train travels from Station A to Station B at a constant speed of 120 mph for 2.5 hours. For the final leg of the journey, due to track maintenance, the train's speed is reduced by 25% for another 1.5 hours until it reaches Station C. What is the total distance covered by the train from Station A to Station C?

A 435 miles
YOUR CHOICE • CORRECT check_circle
B 405 miles
C 450 miles
D 395 miles

Insight Status

Correct

Detailed Solution

Step 1: Distance AB = 120 mph × 2.5 h = 300 miles.

Step 2: Maintenance speed = 120 mph × (1 - 0.25) = 90 mph.

Step 3: Distance BC = 90 mph × 1.5 h = 135 miles.

Step 4: Total = 300 + 135 = 435 miles.
