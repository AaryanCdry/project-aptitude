# Project Brief: AptitudePro Assessment Platform ## 1. Executive Summary AptitudePro is a high-fidelity cognitive assessment and growth platform designed to help students identify their cognitive strengths and provide educators with data-driven insights. The platform moves beyond simple testing into "outcome tracking" and "deep analytics," offering a personalized journey from initial assessment to certified achievement. ## 2. Target Audience * **Students (Primary):** Individuals seeking to understand their cognitive profile (Quantitative, Verbal, Logical, Spatial) and improve through adaptive practice. * **Administrators/Teachers (Secondary):** Educational leads managing cohorts, scheduling assessments, and analyzing collective performance data. ## 3. Core Product Pillars * **Adaptive Testing:** A distraction-free, "flow-state" testing environment that adjusts difficulty in real-time. * **Deep Analytics:** Longitudinal growth tracking and granular cognitive insights (Domain Mastery, Cognitive Profile). * **Administrative Control:** Full-lifecycle management of students, cohorts, and proctored assessment sessions. * **Verified Achievement:** Formal certification of results with authenticated sharing capabilities. ## 4. Key Screens & User Flows ### A. Student Experience 1. **Student Dashboard:** Central hub showing "Cognitive Profile," "Domain Mastery," "Score Trends," and "Recent Activity." 2. **Adaptive Test Interface:** Optimized for focus with a question map, progress tracking, and clear interaction patterns. 3. **Review Mode:** A learning-focused view providing step-by-step solutions and cognitive skill insights for completed tests. 4. **Test Results & Insights:** Immediate post-test breakdown with performance analytics. 5. **Growth & Certification:** Longitudinal timeline of progress and a preview for generating official certificates. ### B. Administrative Experience 1. **Admin Dashboard:** High-level overview of cohort performance and system health. 2. **Cohort Management:** Tools for organizing groups of students and monitoring their collective progress. 3. **Student Enrollment:** Flow for adding students via manual entry, CSV upload, or email invitation. 4. **Assessment Scheduling:** Interface for planning test dates, setting parameters (duration, difficulty), and assigning cohorts. 5. **Live Proctoring Dashboard:** Real-time monitoring of active test sessions with alerts for suspicious behavior. 6. **Assessment Analytics:** Aggregated data visualizations showing performance trends across tests and cohorts. ### C. Account & Support 1. **User Profile Management:** Personal information, preferences, and account security. 2. **Billing & Subscription:** Plan management, usage tracking (assessment quotas), and invoicing history. 3. **Help & Support Center:** Search-first knowledge base and multi-channel support access. ## 5. Design System: "Cognitive Modern" * **Primary Color:** #4f46e5 (Indigo) - Communicates trust, intelligence, and professional growth. * **Typography:** Geist - A clean, modern sans-serif optimized for readability in data-heavy environments. * **Visual Language:** Minimalist cards, high-contrast primary actions, and purposeful use of whitespace to reduce cognitive load. * **Components:** Standardized TopAppBar and SideNavBar for consistent navigation across all user roles. ## 6. Technical Requirements & Integrations * **Responsive Design:** Optimized for Desktop (primary) with adaptive layouts for tablet/mobile. * **Real-time Monitoring:** WebSocket-based live proctoring. * **Data Security:** Encrypted login history and secure credential management. * **Certificate Generation:** SVG-to-PDF generation for official, shareable credentials.
