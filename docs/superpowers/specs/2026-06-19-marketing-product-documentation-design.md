# AptiLead Marketing Product Documentation Design

**Date:** June 19, 2026  
**Status:** Approved

## Objective

Create an accurate product reference for AptiLead’s digital marketing team so that team members can understand the product, explain its value to different audiences, and avoid unsupported claims.

## Audience

The primary audience is the digital marketing team. Secondary readers may include product, sales enablement, and internal stakeholders.

## Approved Positioning

AptiLead is an employability and recruitment platform connecting:

- Students building and proving aptitude skills
- Colleges managing learning, assessments, and outcomes
- Mentors monitoring learner performance
- Companies discovering and screening candidates
- Platform administrators managing institutions

The central product story is:

> **Assessment → Improvement → Certification → Opportunity**

## Deliverables

### 1. Marketing Product Brief

A concise, plain-language reference covering:

- Product overview and elevator pitch
- Problems solved
- Target users
- Stakeholder value
- Core journeys
- Key capabilities
- Differentiators
- Messaging pillars
- Marketing-safe claims
- Current product status
- Evidence-based future direction

### 2. Detailed Product PRD

A comprehensive reference covering:

- Product vision, objectives, and boundaries
- Role-based capabilities
- Adaptive assessments
- Question management
- Scheduling and final exams
- Scoring, analytics, progression, and certificates
- College operations
- Proctoring
- Job discovery
- Company recruitment
- Security and integrations
- Limitations
- Success indicators
- Code-evidenced future capabilities

## Accuracy Model

Every significant capability is described using one of three statuses:

- **Available:** confirmed by an implemented workflow
- **Partially Available:** implemented incompletely or dependent on configuration
- **Planned:** explicitly indicated by disabled flags, placeholders, comments, or unfinished interfaces

The documents do not invent roadmap dates, pricing, customer counts, performance statistics, accreditations, compliance claims, or unsupported AI capabilities.

## Future-Capability Rule

Future direction is restricted to capabilities explicitly indicated in the codebase:

- Webcam-based face-detection proctoring
- Expanded audio anomaly detection
- Persisted feature flags
- Platform announcements
- Notification preferences and alerts
- Expanded college-profile controls
- Persisted API-key administration
- Broader platform commercial settings

## Tone

The writing uses non-technical language suitable for marketers while preserving enough product detail to answer common questions. Technical architecture is summarised without exposing secrets or unnecessary implementation detail.

## Source of Truth

The documentation is derived from the repository state and recent product changes as of June 19, 2026. Where an interface and its underlying behaviour differ, the implemented behaviour takes precedence and the discrepancy is documented.
