# AptiLead Marketing Product Brief

**Document purpose:** Give the digital marketing team a clear, accurate understanding of the product, its audiences, its value, and the claims that can safely be used in campaigns.

**Product:** AptiLead  
**Category:** Employability assessment, college talent development, and recruitment platform  
**Document date:** June 19, 2026

## 1. Product in One Sentence

AptiLead helps colleges measure and improve student aptitude, helps students demonstrate job readiness, and helps employers connect with candidates through one role-based platform.

## 2. Elevator Pitch

AptiLead brings the employability journey into one connected system. Students practise aptitude skills through adaptive assessments, follow their progress, complete institution-assigned tests, earn verifiable certificates, discover jobs, and apply to partner opportunities. Mentors and college leaders can manage learners, schedule assessments, monitor outcomes, identify students who need support, and review performance at class, department, batch, and college level. Employers can publish opportunities, receive structured student applications, and manage candidate shortlisting.

The product connects four stages that are often spread across unrelated tools:

> **Assessment → Improvement → Certification → Opportunity**

## 3. The Problem AptiLead Solves

### For students

- Aptitude preparation is often generic and disconnected from individual performance.
- Students may not know their strongest and weakest aptitude domains.
- Practice, formal assessment, certification, and job discovery are commonly handled on different platforms.
- Students need clearer evidence of readiness when applying for opportunities.

### For colleges

- Student assessment data can be fragmented across spreadsheets and isolated testing tools.
- Administrators need structured control over colleges, departments, classes, batches, academic years, staff, and students.
- Mentors need an efficient way to identify at-risk students and monitor progress.
- Placement-readiness activity is difficult to connect with actual recruitment opportunities.

### For employers

- Early-career candidate screening can require significant manual effort.
- Recruiters need structured candidate context beyond a basic application.
- Companies need a direct channel for reaching students from participating institutions.

## 5. Core Product Journey

### Student journey

1. The student is enrolled by a college or creates an eligible account.
2. The student takes an adaptive practice assessment.
3. AptiLead records domain scores, overall performance, percentile, level, points, and test history.
4. The dashboard highlights cognitive performance, score trends, domain mastery, and the current learning stage.
5. The student completes assessments and final exams assigned by the college.
6. A passing final-exam score can generate a tiered, verifiable certificate.
7. The student discovers degree-relevant jobs and AptiLead partner opportunities.
8. The student applies to partner jobs and tracks application status.

### College journey

1. A platform administrator creates and manages a college.
2. The college principal structures departments, classes, batches, and academic years.
3. Staff members and students are added manually or through bulk enrolment.
4. Admins and mentors manage a question bank and create scheduled assessments.
5. Students complete tests while the system records results and proctoring signals.
6. College teams analyse results, identify at-risk learners, and review top performers.
7. Final exams connect assessment outcomes to certificate issuance.

### Employer journey

1. A company registers and creates its company profile.
2. A recruiter publishes a full-time, internship, or contract opportunity.
3. The recruiter may specify eligible degrees, location, compensation, deadline, requirements, and a minimum aptitude score.
4. Students apply from within AptiLead.
5. The application includes a snapshot of relevant student and aptitude information.
6. The recruiter reviews applications and marks candidates as applied, shortlisted, or rejected.

## 7. Product Differentiators

### One connected employability journey

AptiLead does not stop at test delivery. It connects practice, institutional assessment, performance evidence, certification, job discovery, and partner recruitment.

### Multiple stakeholders, one system

Students, mentors, HODs, principals, platform administrators, and recruiters each receive a dashboard designed around their responsibilities.

### Adaptive rather than purely static practice

Question difficulty changes as the student answers, allowing practice to respond to current performance instead of presenting every learner with the same sequence.

### Institution-aware operations

The product models colleges, departments, classes, batches, and academic years, making it suitable for organised academic deployment rather than only individual consumer use.

### Performance-informed recruitment

Partner-job applications can include an aptitude and academic snapshot, giving recruiters structured context at the time of application.

### Verifiable outcomes

Final-exam certificates use unique verification codes and a public verification flow. Certificates can also be revoked when necessary.

## 8. Messaging Pillars

### Pillar 1: Measure readiness

Understand student aptitude through adaptive practice, scheduled assessments, final exams, and domain-level analytics.

### Pillar 2: Improve with direction

Help students see strengths, weak areas, progress trends, and the next learning stage rather than practising without feedback.

### Pillar 3: Manage at scale

Give college teams structured control over learners, staff, cohorts, assessments, and reports.

### Pillar 4: Prove achievement

Turn final assessment performance into tiered, publicly verifiable certificates.

### Pillar 5: Connect talent to opportunity

Bring job discovery and partner-company applications into the same platform where aptitude development happens.

## 10. Claims Requiring Qualification

Use these only with the stated qualification:

| Topic | Safe qualification |
|---|---|
| AI | AI assists with question generation, PDF question extraction, and explanation generation. Do not describe the entire platform as AI-driven. |
| Proctoring | AptiLead records browser-focus, tab-switch, and audio-related signals. Webcam-based face detection is planned, not currently implemented. |
| Mobile apps | The project includes Android/iOS packaging. Do not claim public app-store availability unless releases are confirmed separately. |
| Job listings | External job discovery depends on a configured third-party job-search API. Partner opportunities are managed directly in AptiLead. |
| Certificates | Certificates are generated from qualifying final-exam performance. Do not claim external accreditation unless separately established. |
| Recruitment screening | Recruiters can view structured application snapshots and aptitude data. Do not claim automated hiring decisions or predictive hiring accuracy. |
| Security/compliance | Role-based access and Supabase authentication are implemented. Do not claim named compliance certifications without independent confirmation. |

## 11. Current Product Status

### Available

- Role-based dashboards and access
- College academic structure and enrolment
- Adaptive practice and scheduled assessments
- Question-bank and assessment management
- Student analytics and progress
- Leaderboards, levels, points, badges, and certificates
- External job discovery when configured
- Company signup, profiles, job posting, and application management
- Responsive web interfaces

### Partially available or configuration-dependent

- AI question features require configured model credentials.
- External job search requires a configured provider API key.
- Proctoring currently uses behavioural and audio-related signals; advanced webcam verification is not active.
- Mobile packaging exists, while production distribution depends on deployment and app-store release processes.
- Some global and college settings interfaces are present but are not fully connected to persistent configuration.
- Company recruitment requires the corresponding database migration to be deployed.

## 14. Internal Marketing Guidance

- Lead with outcomes and connected workflows, not isolated feature lists.
- Separate the student, college, and recruiter value propositions when targeting campaigns.
- Treat the college as the primary organisational buyer unless a different commercial model is formally approved.
- Present partner recruitment as an extension of employability development.
- Clearly label planned capabilities in decks, proposals, and landing pages.
- Do not publish pricing, adoption numbers, accuracy rates, customer logos, or roadmap dates without separate business approval.
